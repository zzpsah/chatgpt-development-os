## Closed capability

P17 evaluates one exact compiled P16 plan step against:
- current repository freshness;
- dependency completion;
- capability evidence;
- exact-step authorization;
- Security Gate evidence;
- verification-path presence;
- plan and completion-evidence integrity.

Outcomes are `READY`, `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, `NEEDS_APPROVAL`, `BLOCKED`, or `STOP`, with `authority: UNCHANGED`, `authorization: UNCHANGED`, and `execution: NONE` preserved.

`READY` remains eligibility only. It is never permission, execution, or completion evidence.
